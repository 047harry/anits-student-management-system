1. System Architecture & Role Model

👨‍🏫 ANITS Faculty Role

Faculty members log in to an executive command roster:

  • View full roster of all students across ANITS engineering branches.
  • Full CRUD capability: Add new students, update marks/CGPA, or delete records.
  • Aggregate analytics: Mean CGPA, attendance warnings (<75% quota).
  • Review completed timed assessment scores.

👨‍🎓 ANITS Student Role

Enforces strict privacy boundaries via JWT authorization:

  • Students can only view their own profile.
  • ANITS Digital B.Tech ID badge with 12-digit Roll Number / USN.
  • Personal CGPA and attendance telemetry gauge.
  • Direct access to the Timed Assessment Examination Chamber.
